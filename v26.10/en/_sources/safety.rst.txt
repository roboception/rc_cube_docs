Intended use

The

rc_cube

 is an industrial processing unit intended to be used in combination
with a 3D camera for data acquisition (e.g., stereo images), for
integration into automation and robotics applications. Its intended
purpose is the acquisition and processing of image data and the
provision of results (e.g. 3D data, detected poses) to a connected
robot controller or host system.

Warning: The

  rc_cube

   is only intended for stationary installation.

The

rc_cube

 is designed and intended to be operated in a trusted environment with
a direct, point-to-point (1:1) connection between the device and the
connected camera, robot controller, or host system. In this
configuration, the devices are not intended to be connected to, or
made accessible from, an open or untrusted network.

Several interfaces required for interoperability with robot
controllers and industrial systems rely on established industry
standards or vendor-specific protocols that do not support encryption
or authentication. Enabling these interfaces outside a trusted,
isolated network may expose the system to cybersecurity risks and is
outside the intended use of the product.

The operator is responsible for ensuring that the operating
environment matches these conditions, including physical access
control to the device, network isolation, and secure configuration of
the host system where applicable. Operating the product outside its
intended use, in particular by exposing unsecured interfaces to an
untrusted network, may compromise the security and reliability of the
system and is not covered by the product's cybersecurity risk
assessment.
